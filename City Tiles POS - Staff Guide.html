Welcome & What This Is

Basics

City Tiles POS is the shop’s billing system. It runs on a Windows computer and is used to:

  • Ring up sales and print receipts on the 80 mm thermal printer.
  • Take payments — cash, bank transfer, or on credit (customer account).
  • Keep track of customers, products, and daily reports.
  • Handle returns and record partial payments.
Good to knowYou do not need to be “technical” to use this. Follow the steps in order and you’ll be fine. When in doubt, ask your manager.
2

First‑Time Setup (on a new computer)

Install
  1. Run the installer your manager gave you — City Tiles POS_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe (or the .msi file).
  2. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC.” This is normal for our installer. Click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Follow the prompts. The app opens when finished. Needs internet once on first launch to set up the browser engine; after that it works fully offline.
  4. Sign in with the account your manager created for you.
Heads‑upThe “Windows protected your PC” message is not a virus warning about our software — it simply means the app is not from a big commercial publisher. “Run anyway” is safe.
3

Signing In

Daily
  1. Open City Tiles POS from the desktop shortcut or Start menu.
  2. Type your email and password, then click Sign in.
  3. Admins land on the dashboard. Cashiers go straight to the sales screen.
  4. Very first use of a brand‑new system: the owner creates the first admin account (email + password). After that, staff are added by the admin.
Forgot your password?Ask your admin to reset it — they use the Cashiers page → reset password. There is no self‑service reset, by design.
4

Making a Sale (your everyday job)

Core
  1. On the POS screen, find the product by searching its name or tapping it in the list.
  2. Tap a product to add it to the cart. Use + / − to change the quantity.
  3. If the customer has a deal, enter a Discount amount in rupees (Rs.).
  4. Choose how they pay: Cash Bank Credit.
  5. If you pick Credit, select the customer so the amount is billed to their account.
  6. Click Complete Sale.
  7. The receipt prints on the 80 mm thermal printer — hand it to the customer.
Payment methods, simplyCash = paid now in cash. Bank = paid now by card/bank transfer. Credit = customer pays later (their account is billed).
5

Customer Accounts & Credit Sales

Credit
  • Credit sales are recorded against a customer, not against cash in hand.
  • Always make sure the correct customer is selected before completing a credit sale.
  • Customers and their outstanding balances are managed under Customers (admin) and tracked in Invoices.
6

Recording Payments & Part Payments

Invoices
  1. Open Invoices to see all sales.
  2. Open an unpaid or credit invoice → Record payment.
  3. Enter the amount received and save. You can pay part now and the rest later.
  4. When a payment fully clears, click Payment cleared so the record stays tidy.
7

Returns & Refunds

Returns
  1. Open Returns from the menu.
  2. Find the original sale/invoice and process the return.
  3. The system handles the refund or credit automatically.
  4. Unsure about the refund amount? Ask a manager before confirming.
8

End of Day — Reports

Admin
  1. Admins: open Reports to see the day’s sales.
  2. Click Export to save a spreadsheet (CSV) for accounting/records.
  3. The report lists: invoice number, date, customer, payment method, subtotal, discount, total, and amount paid.
9

Inventory & Customers

Admin
InventoryAdd or edit products and keep stock counts up to date so the POS shows what’s available.
CustomersAdd customer accounts and review balances — important for credit sales.
10

Managing Staff

Admin

Use the Cashiers page to: approve new cashier accounts, suspend a cashier, or reset a cashier’s password.

11

Backups & Your Data

Safety
  • All data lives on this computer in the CityTiles data folder — the system keeps automatic backups.
  • Do not delete that folder.
  • Admins can export data as an extra safety copy; keep the export somewhere safe.

Troubleshooting

Help
ProblemWhat to do
“Windows protected your PC”That’s SmartScreen. Click More info → Run anyway. Safe to proceed.
App won’t open after installRestart the computer. Make sure you allowed the install, and that it finished. Try opening from the Start menu.
Printer is blank / nothing printsCheck the 80 mm roll is loaded and not jammed, the printer is powered and connected (USB), and it is set as the default thermal printer.
Forgot passwordAsk your admin to reset it (Cashiers page → reset password).
Screen frozen / not respondingClose and reopen the app. If it happens often, tell your admin.
“No internet” messageOnly the very first launch needs internet once. After that it works offline.

Good Habits

Tips
  • Always check the customer name on credit sales before completing.
  • Double‑check the discount before you hit Complete Sale.
  • Log out at the end of your shift.
  • Don’t switch off the computer while a receipt is printing.
